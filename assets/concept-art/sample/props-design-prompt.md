# Props / tree authored design brief

Created before modeling as an original SVG construction and palette study, not an AI image.

Design intent: reproduce the reference's grounded, human-scale Wuxia village craftsmanship. A mature, split-trunk locust/banyan has an asymmetric layered 12 m crown, climbing woodgrain and visible reaching branches; no magical glow. Root buttresses stay within 1.5 m of origin. A precise 4 × 0.3 × 1.2 m fence uses aged mortised rails, irregular staves, and small repair braces. The small prop family is a braced packing crate, stave barrel with forged hoops, subdued cinnabar paper lantern on a crooked timber pole, a masonry well with winch and bucket, and a plank market table with cloth and trays. Each object fits within 2 × 2 m. Materials use original painted/baked PNGs: umber timber, warm neutral stone, muted jade foliage, restrained warm red paper. Model front is −Y; origins sit at footprint center and ground level.

Reference inspected: assets/concept-art/starter-village-wide-v1.png. No third-party content.
